# Omarchy themes studio

Where the videos for Omarchy themes get made: the intro that plays before a theme's background still, and the loop that keeps that still moving. The finished clips for the built-in themes live in [omacom/omarchy-themes-intros](https://github.com/omacom/omarchy-themes-intros) and [omacom/omarchy-themes-loops](https://github.com/omacom/omarchy-themes-loops); this repository is for making them, and for letting anyone make their own.

**Status:** being designed. The direction being tried is a scene: a small description and a few pictures that a player draws live, with video kept for what a scene cannot do. One background plays as a scene in a throwaway player.

- [`docs/fixed-ground.md`](docs/fixed-ground.md) - what Omarchy fixes for any video: where it goes, when it plays, its format, and how it ships. Everything else is open.
- [`kanagawa/1-kanagawa/`](kanagawa/1-kanagawa/about.md) - a first try at opening up one still: the picture cut into its parts and inks by a recipe, with room for a painting where the recipe falls short.
- [`catppuccin/1-totoro/`](catppuccin/1-totoro/about.md) - the first scene: the shipped Totoro loop repeated by eleven small pieces, and what it costs next to the video.
- `nord/0-black-moon/scene.jsonc` - a draft scene that moves areas of the still with effects instead of cut pieces. Its pictures are not made.
